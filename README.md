# ⚽ Timestamped Football Odds incl. Pinnacle — 44 European Leagues: Eredivisie, Super Lig, Scottish Premiership + 41 More (free sample)

**Free sample: 47 matches out of 92,615, one or more from each of the 44 leagues.** Same files, same
columns, same formats as the full product. Only the number of matches differs.

Most football odds datasets give you one closing number per match. The full product gives you
**132,116,120 timestamped quotes** from 5 named bookmakers, pre-match and in-play, with the score
and the match clock on the in-play quotes, plus the opening and the closing of every book and market with the
exact time each was recorded, and 4,705,054 dated snapshots from 37 more bookmakers.

## Said plainly

Stated up front rather than discovered after paying.

- **Not collected for four leagues:** Belarus and Russia have no Betfair or Betsson, Moldova and North
  Macedonia no Unibet.
- **Russia since March 2022 and Belarus since its 2022 season:** no Bet365, no Unibet, no events and no
  minute-by-minute statistics in the source. Pinnacle, the other bookmakers' snapshots, the match sheet and the
  team statistics continue (lineups too for Russia).
- **Gaps in time:** Pinnacle 2023-09 to 2023-11 and 2026-07 to 2026-08; Unibet 2026-07 to 2026-08; Betfair (Sportsbook) 2024-11 to 2025-08 and 2026-07 to 2026-08; Betsson 2026-06 to 2026-08; minute stats 2018-01 to 2019-09 and 2026-06 to 2026-07. In July and August 2026, Bet365 is the only complete book.
- **A closing quote is the last pre-match price recorded.** Median gap to kickoff on FT 1X2: Pinnacle 5 min, Betfair 6 min, Betsson 11 min, Bet365 27 min, Unibet 3.0 h.
  That is why every book has its own closing time.
- **The other bookmakers' table is a dated snapshot, not a closing price:** the source keeps three
  snapshots per book (first price, around kickoff, last price). Check `Minutes_Before_Kickoff`.
- **Lineups are absent or almost absent for 15 leagues** (Albania, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Georgia, Israel, Kazakhstan, Luxembourg, Latvia, Moldova, North Macedonia, Malta, Montenegro, Northern Ireland, Wales)
  and partial for 9 more (Belarus 7 %, Estonia 11 %, Faroe Islands 8 %, Hungary 9 %, Ireland 10 %, Iceland 9 %, Lithuania 12 %, Scotland 39 %, Serbia 28 %).
- **Data starts on 2016-08-28:** the first season of each league is partial (its first matchdays
  are missing). The last seasons stop at 2026-09-30.
- **184 source events were removed** (not played with the result awarded, duplicate ids, resumptions
  and replays of interrupted matches...), each listed with its evidence in `sample/README.md`. 15 matches
  the source flags as decided by the federation are kept: played to the end, score unchanged.
- **Odds are UTC and come from one provider.** No cross-provider reconciliation is claimed.

taken back (the half-time score is 1-0), and a 1-2 that flickers back to 1-1 for a few seconds at 58'. Quotes are
delivered as the bookmaker sent them, never smoothed: a suspended market is an empty price with `Suspended` = 1.

## Eight tables, one key

Everything joins on `Match_ID`. No fuzzy name matching.

| File | One row per | Key content |
|---|---|---|
| `OtherEurope_Matches.csv` / `.xlsx` | match | season, stage, matchday, leg, kickoff, scores HT / 90' / AET / penalties, aggregate, table positions, formations, managers, referee, venue |
| `OtherEurope_Opening_Closing_Odds.csv` / `.xlsx` | match | open and close per book and market, each with its time |
| `odds_timestamped/` (44 × `.csv.gz`) | price update | book, market, phase, prices, line, score, clock, suspension, UTC, epoch |
| `bookmaker_snapshots/` (44 × `.csv.gz`) | snapshot | 37 more books: first and last pre-match snapshot, with minutes before kickoff |
| `OtherEurope_Team_Stats.csv` / `.xlsx` | match × team | 29 metrics incl. xG |
| `lineups/` (44 × `.csv.gz`) | player × match | starters, subs, shirt, position |
| `events/` (44 × `.csv.gz`) | event | 13 event types with minute and player |
| `minute_stats/` (44 × `.csv.gz`) | stat × minute | running stats minute by minute |

Plus `OtherEurope_Coverage.csv` / `.xlsx`: one row per league and season, coverage of every table and book in %.

The complete column dictionary, the seasons and stages of every league, the coverage tables and the integrity
notes are in `sample/README.md`, the README shipped with the product.

**Nothing a spreadsheet can reinterpret.** Scores, minutes and shoot-outs are separate integer
columns. Timestamps are full ISO 8601 with the T and the Z, plus an epoch. Formations are digits
(4231 = 4-2-3-1), so 4-4-2 never becomes 4 April 2002. Seasons read 2016/17 or 2019. Every cell
was audited against Excel auto-conversion patterns at write time: zero risky cells. A closing price
is never an in-play price.
